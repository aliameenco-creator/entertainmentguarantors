Custom theme for Entertainment Guarantors Malta. Layouts are generated from the archived original site (entertainmentguarantors.net) and keep its URLs: /, /solutions, /about and /contact.

== Setup ==
After activating, open any wp-admin page and click "Complete Entertainment Guarantors setup" (post-name permalinks without a trailing slash, site title, Media Library images). Enquiries from the contact form are saved under Enquiries; set the recipient under Settings > General.
