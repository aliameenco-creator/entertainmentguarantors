# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Pages are now normal editable WordPress pages: setup creates Home, Services, Our Team and Partners and Contact (block editor, all blocks valid) at the original URLs, sets Home as the homepage and creates the Main menu (Appearance > Menus). Existing pages are never overwritten.
- Footer contacts editable under Appearance > Customize > Footer contacts; logo replaceable via Site Identity.
- Redesign with the same photos, video, posters and copy: Instrument Serif + Manrope typography, transparent-to-glass header, gold accents from the logo, dark film slider with progress bar, stacked cards, framed parallax image, navy call to action, four-column footer, greyscale-to-colour partner logos.
- Setup now also switches date-based or trailing-slash permalinks to /%postname% when no real posts would move.

## 0.1.0
- First release: homepage (looping hero video with still fallback, poster slider, stacked cards, parallax image), Services, Our Team and Partners and Contact at the original URLs (/, /solutions, /about, /contact); /home redirects to /.
- Original logo, colours, copy and navigation; Wix template leftovers (LUMINU logo, placeholder contact details and social links, dead legal links) are not used.
- Contact form (new: the original site had none), saved as Enquiries and emailed; one-click setup; Media Library import; GitHub release updater.
