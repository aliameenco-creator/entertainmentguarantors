Services and Protection for ALL Film and Television Budgets and Investments

What We Do

We take a hands-on approach with all departments, visiting sets and deploying a consistent monitor of expenditure against budget, focusing on interaction with the finance and production team that also coincides with our ongoing risk management programme.

01

Bond Packages

We offer different options that can allow us to discount the total cost to the production company.

This may provide savings to meet the budgetary constraints and requirements of the producer.

We have a flexible approach to the bonding process which translates to a product tailored to the needs and requirements of the producer and financiers.

Our approach is not just about financial oversight—it’s about strategic collaboration, ensuring productions run smoothly from pre-production to final delivery.

02

Risk Management

We meticulously analyze budgets, schedules, and production risks to offer a tailored completion bond that protects investors.

This service is usually provided on smaller budget films, where the cost of a bond is considered prohibitive, but the financiers require an independent third party to carry out the required due-diligence and monitoring of the production.

We actively oversee productions, working closely with financiers and producers to keep projects on track and on budget.

03

Post-Completion Bonds

This type of bond provides protection in the same manner as a full completion bond but only once filming is completed and the film is ‘in the can’.

The film has probably run out of finances, but needs finishing. Producers will seek to raise additional funds to complete the film and the investors want a guarantee the film will be completed.

04

Tax Credit Only Bonding

Many Banks and Investors will discount Producer’s Tax Credits but require certainty that the film will comply with legislation and that the film is made in accordance with the opinion letter provided by the Auditor on which they base the risk.

Film Insurance

Entertainment Guarantors provides a comprehensive suite of insurance solutions designed to protect film and television productions across every stage of the production lifecycle.

While Completion Bonds remain a core insurance-backed guarantee—ensuring projects are delivered on time, on budget, and substantially in accordance with approved materials—our wider insurance offering extends beyond delivery protection to encompass the broader risk environment faced by producers, financiers, and distributors.

We arrange full Production Insurance coverage tailored to the operational realities of filming, safeguarding productions against unforeseen interruptions, physical losses, and liabilities that can arise throughout development, principal photography, and post-production.

We also provide Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance, a critical component for distribution and release. E&O coverage protects against claims relating to intellectual property, defamation, rights clearance, and contractual exposures, ensuring that completed projects can proceed confidently into the marketplace.

Entertainment Guarantors works closely with producers, financiers, and insurance partners to structure robust, internationally recognised coverage that supports secure investment, protects creative vision, and enables smooth delivery and exploitation of film and television assets.
